M0b-3 A/B scheduling

Two variants scheduled gaplessly on one timeline (src.start(t, 0.12 + …), 5 ms seam ramps over the bit-identical 20 ms overlap), then A/B'd every 100 ms with 10 ms linear gain ramps. An AudioWorklet tap (ScriptProcessor fallback) records per-block RMS of each variant, first/second-difference of the mix and DC mirrors of the two switch gains, so the page can measure planned-vs-actual switch time, voices alive and click proxies.
