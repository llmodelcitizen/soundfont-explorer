"Soundfont Explorer GM diagnostic" (sfp-diagnostic.mid)

Generated deterministically by songs/tools/make_diagnostic.py for this project.
It is not a musical work: it steps through all 128 General MIDI programs, the
GM drum map, pitch bends and controller sweeps so every synth bank can be
checked for coverage.
